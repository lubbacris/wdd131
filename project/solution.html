Frequently Asked Questions

System costs range from R80,000 to R250,000 depending on size and requirements. We offer financing options to make it affordable.

With battery backup, your solar system will continue to power essential circuits. Without batteries, the system shuts down for safety but resumes immediately when grid power returns.

Most installations are completed within 2-3 days, including 1 day for assessment and planning.

Solar systems require minimal maintenance. We recommend annual inspections and occasional panel cleaning.
