Site Name

Solar System Rollout

This name clearly communicates the project's purpose - rolling out solar energy systems. It's straightforward, professional, and easy to remember. The name suggests action and implementation, which aligns with our goal of actively deploying clean energy solutions.

Optional domain availability: solarsystemrollout.co.za

Site Purpose

The Solar System Rollout website serves as the central information hub for our clean energy initiative targeting urban residential centers in South Africa. The site will provide information about solar energy benefits and facilitate the process for residents to request assessments and installations for their homes.

Scenarios

These are questions our target audience (urban homeowners) might ask:

  • "How much can I actually save on my electricity bill by switching to solar, and what is the total installation cost?"
  • "What happens during load-shedding if I have a solar system? Will I still have power?"
  • "Installing solar systems are expensive. Is the government subsidizing this or giving us some form of financial aid?"

Color Schema

The color palette is designed to reflect energy, trust, and sustainability:

Primary
#2E86AB

Primary Blue: Used for main headings, navigation bar, and primary buttons. Represents trust, reliability, and energy.

Secondary
#F39C12

Secondary Orange: Used for accents, CTAs, and highlights. Represents the sun, energy, and action.

Typography

One font selected for readability and professional appearance:

Roboto - For All Text

This clean, modern sans-serif font will be used for all text elements including headings, body text, navigation, and buttons. It provides strong visual hierarchy and professional appearance while maintaining excellent readability.

Font Usage Examples:

  • Headings (Roboto Bold): Main page titles and section headings
  • Body text (Roboto Regular): All paragraphs, lists, and general content
  • Navigation (Roboto): Menu items and links
